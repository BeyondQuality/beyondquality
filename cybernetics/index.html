Chapter 1: Adaptation Time (The 45-Second Hotel Shower)

Imagine adjusting an old hotel shower where turning the tap takes 45 seconds to change the water temperature at the showerhead. You feel freezing water, crank it hot, wait 15 seconds, find it still freezing, crank it further, get scalded, and yank it back to cold. You never stabilise because the delay in your feedback loop blinds you to the effect of each adjustment until it is too late.

A product company faces the same lag constraint against a changing market. Its round-trip duration from noticing a market need, through Discovery (writing the Spec) and Build, to observing real users is its adaptation time. Even when market diagnosis is 100% accurate and code has zero bugs, starting work today means delivering into the world of today + adaptation time.

Chapter 1 Lesson

Delay alone wrecks steering: when exhaustive upfront study or oversized build batches stretch your adaptation time past the market's pace of change, faultless research still ships an answer to yesterday's problem; keeping pace requires shipping thin vertical slices rather than rushing people.
